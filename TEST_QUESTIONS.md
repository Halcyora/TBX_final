# TBX Finance Assistant - Test Questions

## Easy Questions (10)

1. How many accounts do we have?
2. List all the banks in the database
3. What is the total number of transactions?
4. Show me all account types
5. How many accounts belong to HDFC?
6. What is the account number for the first account?
7. List all transactions from 2024
8. How many accounts are active?
9. What is the currency used in the database?
10. Show me one transaction example

## Medium Questions (10)

1. What is the average balance across all accounts?
2. How much total money is in all HDFC accounts?
3. Which bank has the most accounts?
4. What is the sum of all transaction amounts in January 2024?
5. How many transactions occurred per bank?
6. What is the highest balance among all accounts?
7. Show me the top 5 accounts by balance
8. How many accounts have a balance greater than 100,000?
9. What is the average transaction amount?
10. Which account has had the most transactions?

## Hard Questions (10)

1. Calculate the net change in balance for each account based on its transactions
2. Which account received the largest single transaction and from which bank?
3. What percentage of total available balance does each bank represent?
4. Find accounts with declining balances over time
5. Calculate the monthly transaction volume for each bank
6. Which customer/account has the highest transaction frequency?
7. What is the average balance per account type, broken down by bank?
8. Identify any unusual transaction patterns (outliers in amount or frequency)
9. Calculate the cumulative balance growth for the top 3 accounts
10. What percentage of accounts have balances below the average balance?
